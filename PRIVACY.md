# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-06_

QR Code Generator does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

## What the extension accesses

- **The current tab's URL**, only when you click the toolbar icon, to pre-fill the URL field.
- **Your settings** (UTM values, custom parameters, the "remember" choice and the error correction level) are saved with `chrome.storage.local` on your device so they are there next time. Untick "Remember parameters" to stop saving parameter values. Removing the extension deletes them.

## What it does not do

- It makes no network requests. QR codes are generated locally in your browser.
- It has no analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
- It does not read page content. The script injected on click only displays the extension's own panel.
- Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party.
